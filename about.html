About

Last updated 22/05/2026

This site is a bit of a centralized portfolio of my interests over time. Primarily, this shows most of my software projects in one place, making it a little easier to find them all along with anything else that may be associated with them.

Images in the gallery are usually produced using Blender, though some are produced through more specialized experiments. Research into graphical technologies and the extremes of parallel compute and concurrency is my personal interest.

If you came here for help with the cipher on my discord status, I'm afraid there's no help for you here. :)

Used in this site

Site is built and statically compiled using Svelte/SvelteKit and its Static Side Generation Adapter. Additionally, Yarn is used for package management and Lucide as an iconkit on the projects page.

Style code, components and images are custom.
